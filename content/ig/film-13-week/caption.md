## CAPTION

Claude tracked the customer I had already forgotten.

Now the week after a deal closes does not end with the founder moving on to the next one,
because the next one is not where churn starts. Churn starts on day one, when nobody checks if
the customer actually got what they paid for.

Seven days, one customer, and I did not open the dashboard once:

Day one it turns the deal notes into an onboarding checklist, so nothing I promised gets
dropped. Days two and three it watches engagement against that checklist, and flags the one
account that is already behind. Days four and five it drafts the check-in from the signal, in
my own words, so I sound like I noticed. Days six and seven the week-end summary arrives with
who is healthy, who is not, and what I send next.

It does not send anything and it does not replace my judgement. It builds, it watches, it
flags, and I decide.

This is the Ultron platform, one input and a team that runs behind it.
