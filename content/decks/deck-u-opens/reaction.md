# Reaction opener - deck-u-opens

**Kicker (small, above)**  The stack, counted

**ON SCREEN** (what they read, keep it short)

> Seven tools. I open three.

**YOU SAY** (over the same shot)

> I counted every open for a month. The most expensive one is the one I opened least, and one of them I had forgotten I pay for.

**Then** slide 1, which does NOT repeat this line.

**Pattern** life-contrast (operator's own example, operator)

> Office end at 5 pm. Me at 11 pm

Two clock times, no verb, no claim. The gap is the whole hook and the viewer closes it themselves.
