## LINKEDIN CAPTION  (carousel 1080x1350)

The first hire most founders make is an SDR, and the message that person would send does not
exist yet. AI covers six of these seven roles until the work is real enough to hire for.

- - -

None of these roles are wrong forever. All seven are jobs a company eventually needs and most
of them are jobs I will hire for myself. The question is never whether the role is real, it is
whether the work is real yet, and the honest answer at 3 people is usually no. A hire made
before the work exists does not create the work. It creates a person waiting for you to invent
it, on your payroll, while you feel guilty about it every Friday.

- - -

→ The first SDR. You do not have a repeatable message yet. Hiring someone to send one you have
not found makes the not-finding faster and more expensive.

→ A researcher. 40 companies, who signs at each, what changed this quarter. This is the one
role on the list that never comes back as a hire, because the work does not need a person any
more.

→ A content writer. Nobody can write in your voice from a brief. They can once your voice
exists in writing, so do that part first and the hire gets easy.

→ Sales ops before there are operations. A process you have not written down cannot be
operated by anyone. Write it, run it twice, then hire it.

→ A second founder. Loneliness is not a skills gap. If the reason is that you are tired, the
answer is leverage, and equity is the most expensive way anybody has ever bought company.

- - -

Two of the seven are genuinely hires and I said so on the slides: a chief of staff, which
people make at 3 people and should make at 12, and counsel, which you want the day you sign
something big. The other five are work, not headcount, and the difference matters because
headcount is the one decision on this list you cannot quietly undo in a month. The test I use
now is short. Has this work happened twice, and did it happen the same way both times? If the
answer is no, hiring for it means paying somebody to invent a process while you are still
guessing at it, and then defending their existence to yourself every Friday. If the answer is
yes, hire, and hire faster than you are planning to.
