# Reaction opener - deck-s-quick

**Kicker (small, above)**  The two day feature

**ON SCREEN** (what they read, keep it short)

> Quick features bill you monthly. Forever.

**YOU SAY** (over the same shot)

> I quoted two days. Here is what it actually cost, itemised, including the line that never stops.

**Then** slide 1, which does NOT repeat this line.

**Pattern** future-regret (233K, @saumya1singh)

> If you ignore these skills in 2026, don't complain about your salary in 2027.

Puts a dated price on inaction. It is a threat with a receipt attached, and the date makes it feel like a countdown.

**Keyword** QUICK

Files: `reaction-overlay.png` drops straight over your footage (transparent), `reaction-preview.png` is how it reads.
