# Reaction opener - deck-o-split

**Kicker (small, above)**  What still needs me

**ON SCREEN** (what they read, keep it short)

> Hand over the wrong job and you find out.

**YOU SAY** (over the same shot)

> Seven jobs in my week. I handed over four of them, and the three I kept are the reason the company is still mine.

**Then** slide 1, which does NOT repeat this line.

**Pattern** cautionary (11.0M, #claudeai)

> What happens when you let AI manage your production infrastructure? One developer found out the hard way.

A question about a risky choice, then a person who already paid for the answer. The viewer is not being taught, they are being warned, and a warning is the one thing nobody scrolls past.
