# The operator's hook archive, read off the overlays

61 transparent overlays (720x1280) he supplied as `Hooks_Overlay.zip`. The filenames are
truncated and carry only line one, so the real text was read off the images: the band sits at
rows 215..322, and these contact sheets crop that band from every file, eleven to a sheet, so the
whole archive can be read in six looks instead of sixty one.

The format, without exception:

    LINE 1   THE CLAIM, ALL CAPS, first person, usually a number
    LINE 2   (the payoff, lower case, in brackets)

Measured across the 61: 31 open with "I", 42 carry a digit, 32 name a tool count. Duplicates:
23 and 24 are identical, and THE FREEDOM PYRAMID appears four times (53 to 56), so ~57 are unique.

**The bracket is never a caption.** His strongest ones add a price, an outcome or an attitude:
(they replaced $10K in payroll), (like a real CEO), (they work 24/7 and need no equity), (they
never ask for a raise), (no staff. no office. just software), (and 0 meetings), (before burning
out), (for peace and profit), (with icons). That is the mechanic to copy, and CLAUDE.md 14 is
superseded for this lane by the archive itself.

The ten built materials take their hooks from here by position; see the map in `content/_stack.py`.
