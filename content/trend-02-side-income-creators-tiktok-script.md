## Corrections applied to the trend outline

1. The generated outline peaks on "Imagine earning 1k extra weekly, yes it is totally doable with
   this method". That is an unverifiable income claim. It is the exact pattern TikTok demonetises
   and reports, and we have no data behind the number. It is cut. No earnings figure appears in
   this script unless you give me a real one.
2. The outline is generic side hustle bait with nothing to sell. The honest version of this trend
   for us already exists: the Ultron Creators program, app.51ultron.com/creators, Create then Post
   then Get paid. That is a real Ultron surface, so the clip rides a 6.20M hashtag lane without
   inventing an offer.
3. The trend note says the winners showcase "scams to avoid". That is the engagement driver and it
   is also true content, so beat 2 is a real filter, not bait.

## Audience note, read before you shoot

This clip is deliberately off the founder ICP. It recruits creators, not buyers. It feeds the UGC
funnel and the hashtag lane is 20x bigger than the founder lane, but the followers it brings are
not your pipeline. Ship it as a Creators-funnel clip, not as a GTM clip, and do not judge it by
the same metric as the founder posts.
