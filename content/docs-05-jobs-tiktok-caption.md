## PRIMARY

I used to babysit every long AI task, watching a spinner so I would not lose the run.

Now I close the tab and Claude keeps working. Long jobs run in the background while I move on, and the work is finished when I come back.

Anything that will not finish in seconds becomes a background job: scrape 200 sites, enrich a list, transcribe a call, or a scheduled 9am digest.

One line becomes a mission that spawns child jobs, one per step: research, skill, subagent, sandbox, carousel and a human gate. A live donut tracks every job without a refresh, each one has a state you control, cancel, pause, resume or retry just the step that broke, and nothing sends until you approve it.

This is Background Jobs on the Ultron platform, long-running AI work that runs itself.
