## CAPTION

A customer paid on Monday and by Tuesday I was already chasing the next deal. That is the honest
part, because it is exactly what every founder does: you close one, you invoice one, you chase
the next, and the customer you just won sits in a spreadsheet you have not opened since.
Churn does not start with an email; it starts with silence.

I did not open the dashboard once that week. I did not check whether they logged in or whether
the promise I made in the closing call had been written down. I was not being negligent. I was
doing what every founder does: the next deal is always more urgent than the last one, and the
cost of ignoring the last one does not show up for about ninety days.

- - -

What the seven days looked like, and what I would have missed without the system watching:

→ Day one: the onboarding checklist built from deal notes. I had made four promises in the
closing call and written down two of them. The third was in a note nobody had opened because
the deal was already marked won. The checklist caught the gap before the customer noticed it.

→ Days two and three: engagement watched against that checklist. One account was already
behind because the onboarding link had gone to spam and they had never seen it. A flag on day
three costs one email. A flag on day thirty costs one customer and one referral you never know
you did not get.

→ Days four and five: the check-in drafted from the signal, in my own words, so what went out
sounded like someone who had noticed rather than a template that had been triggered by a rule.
That is the line that matters most to me: the message was mine, the observation was the
system's, and the timing was the only part I would not have managed alone. I read it once,
added one sentence about the call, and sent it.

→ Days six and seven: the summary arrived with who was healthy, who was not, and what I sent
next. One week of watching, one customer I did not lose to silence, and a dashboard I never
opened because the summary came to me.

- - -

That is the whole workflow: it builds, it watches, it flags, it drafts, and I send. It does not
send anything on its own and it does not replace my judgement. Anyone selling you the version
where the software runs the relationship while you sleep is selling you the exact same churn
with extra steps.
