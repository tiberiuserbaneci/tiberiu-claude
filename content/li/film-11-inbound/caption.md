## CAPTION

Three people asked about the product before ten in the morning. Claude answered the one that
mattered at minute fifty eight, and I was in a meeting for the whole hour.

Outbound gets all the attention because it is the part that feels like work. The lead that
arrives on its own is the one I have always handled worst: it lands while I am on a call, it
sits, and by the time I write something back the moment it was asked in has passed. I stopped
treating that hour as admin and watched what actually happens inside it.

- - -

Four things I had wrong about inbound:

→ The form does not say what they are asking. It says what fits in the box. What they actually
want is in the sentence they wrote underneath it, and it is usually a different question.

→ Two of the three were never going to buy, and I would have spent the afternoon being polite
to both. Naming them took fourteen minutes and cost nothing, because the reasons were public.

→ Speed is not the whole thing, but it is most of it. The same reply written tomorrow is a
worse reply, not because the words change but because the question has gone cold.

→ The reply that works answers what they asked. Not what I wish they had asked, which is the
version I write when I am rushing and want to talk about the roadmap.

- - -

The part I did not expect is that none of this is a template. A template answers the question
you predicted. What went out at minute fifty eight answered a specific person about a specific
thing they were worried about, and it referenced something they had written themselves.

I read it once and changed nothing, which has never happened with anything I have automated
before. That is the only reason I trust it: it holds every reply for a yes, and if the draft
were wrong I would see it before anyone else did. Nothing sends itself. It never should, and
any tool that offers to is solving a problem I do not have.

The hour is still an hour. What changed is that I was not in it, and the person who wrote to me
got an answer on the day they were still thinking about it rather than a week later when they
had already talked to someone else. That is the whole margin, and it is not a copywriting
margin or a product margin. It is just being there.
