## CAPTION

Twenty two deals in my pipeline had not moved in a month, and not one of them had said no.
Claude was the first thing to say that out loud.

I had forty one deals open and I had not looked at the list honestly in three weeks. Not
because I did not care about it. Because a real review costs an afternoon I never have, and
every deal in there had a reason to still be in there. That is the whole problem: a pipeline
does not lie to you, it just never volunteers the truth. Every entry survives on the last good
thing that happened in it, and the last good thing does not expire on its own. So I gave the
whole list to Claude with every note, every thread and every date attached, and asked one
question. Which of these would you still be working on Monday morning.

- - -

What came back, and why none of it was obvious from the board:

→ Twenty two had not moved in a month and had never received a no. Nobody says no any more,
they go quiet, and quiet reads exactly like alive on every board I have ever kept. These were
the ones I was still counting.

→ Ten more were a person who liked me and could not sign anything. Every one of them had a
warm last conversation in it, which is the reason they survived three previous reviews. Warm
is not the same as authorised, and only one of those two closes.

→ Nine were real. Not nine that might work, nine with a named person who can sign, a reason
to move this quarter, and something that happened in the last two weeks.

→ One of the nine was closer than everything I had ranked above it. It had been sitting in the
middle of the list the whole time. My own ordering had it eleventh.

- - -

The useful part is not that thirty two deals died. They were already dead, I was just still
carrying them, and carrying a dead deal is not free. It takes the follow up, the mental slot
and the forecast that a live one should have had. Thirty two of those, running quietly against
nine that deserved the whole week. What I actually got back was not a cleaner list. It was
Monday.

It does not close anything and it does not forecast. It reads, it ranks, and I decide.
