## CAPTION

Most AI buries the answer in a wall of text you have to re-read. Claude hands me an editable canvas instead, sorted, scored and ready to ship.

- - -

I stopped copying AI output into a doc to make it usable. Now, when an answer has structure, Claude renders it on a canvas next to the chat. The reply stays one line and the structure becomes a thing I can work: a table I can sort, a score card I can re-rank, a sequence I can re-time, or a blueprint I can edit. I asked it to audit my GTM and it built the whole canvas while the chat reply stayed a single sentence.

- - -

→ I typed audit my GTM and got a live audit canvas: six pipeline stages scored ICP 48, Positioning 32, Website 28, Content 45, Sales 52 and Outbound 35, an overall 41 out of 100, and three critical findings.

→ Every canvas is one of four kinds. A comparison table up to 9 columns, a weighted score card out of 100, a sequence of 7 steps with owners, or a blueprint of 6 editable sections.

→ The canvas is live, not a screenshot. I sort the table, change a weight and the rank moves, edit a cell, all inside the Builder tab.

→ Each finding is a current versus recommended pair. ICP lives in a doc, not enforced becomes one ICP enforced across all GTM, so the fix is obvious.

→ One click gets it out. Copy as markdown, download the CSV, share a link, or push it into a note. Nothing is trapped in the thread.

- - -

The unlock was never a smarter reply. It was a reply with a shape. When the answer is structured, reading it is the slow part, and a canvas you can edit beats a paragraph you have to re-type every time.
