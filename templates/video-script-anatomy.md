# Video Script Anatomy — Creator Tool Stack Reels

Source analysis: peter.visuals "@higgsfield.ai + Claude might be the craziest combo creators are sleeping on."

## Architecture

High-performing creator tool stack Reels follow a FOMO-driven architecture.

### 1. Hook (0-3 sec)
- Contrarian or "sleeping on" framing
- Positions a specific tool combo as hidden knowledge
- Inject specificity immediately — name exact tools, models, protocols

### 2. Body (3-45 sec)
- Demo-driven with screen recordings or generated outputs
- NOT slides or talking heads
- Show, do not tell
- Numbers in overlays, not narration

### 3. Proof / Punch (45-55 sec)
- Result reveal at higher speed than setup
- Before/after contrast or quantity flex ("30 models, one pipeline")

### 4. CTA (55-60 sec)
- Engagement, not sales
- "Comment X for Y" or "Save this for later"
- Never pricing

## Tone Rules

- Direct. No filler.
- No em-dashes, no smart quotes, no ellipsis.
- No AI-tell words: leverage, seamless, optimize, unlock, empower, revolutionary, robust, delve.
- Every sentence declarative.
- Real numbers only.

## LinkedIn Variation Patterns

### Variation A: The Signal
"Most founders are still asking X. That is the wrong question. [Company] just proved [specific number]. But that is not the interesting part."

### Variation B: The Observation
"I watched a 60-second Reel this week that explained more about [topic] than most [professional output]. [Tool A] handled X. [Tool B] handled Y. [Specific metric]. No humans in the middle."

### Variation C: The Take
"[Company/Size] just proved something most [audience] have not internalized yet. [Company] did not build the best [thing]. It built a platform that connects to everyone else's. [List competitors/models]. That is smart. But the real insight is [protocol/chain insight]."

## Cross-Platform Adaptation

| Platform | Format | Hook Style | CTA |
|----------|--------|-----------|-----|
| Instagram Reels | Vertical 9:16, demo-heavy | "Sleeping on" / FOMO | "Save this" / "Tag a founder" |
| LinkedIn | Text-first, carousel or single image | Contrarian / data-led | "Comment X for Y" |
| X/Twitter | Thread format, 5-7 tweets | Hot take / observation | "Delivered to your DMs if you reply" |
| Substack | Long-form, 800-1200 words | Narrative opening | Email signup / forward |
