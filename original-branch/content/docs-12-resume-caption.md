## CAPTION

I gave Claude 240 founder leads and told it to email them. It drafted the whole sequence, then refused to send a single one until I tapped approve.

Most founders are scared to let an AI agent touch outbound, billing or their CRM. I was too. The horror story is always the same: you wake up to an agent that emailed the wrong list, burned the month of credits, or dropped a table while you slept.

- - -

So I traced what actually happens inside Ultron the moment an agent reaches a high-stakes action. It does not just fire and apologize. The turn pauses, a card appears, and nothing runs until I decide. The same gate sits in front of every risky move, the same way every time, no matter which agent triggered it.

- - -

→ Five action types never run alone: sending outbound, spending credits, writing to external tools like your CRM or calendar, deleting or overwriting your own data, and sending legal documents. Everything a founder would sign off on by hand, and nothing else.

→ Each action carries one of three risk levels. Low auto-runs so research and drafts never interrupt you, medium asks the first time then remembers your call, high always asks. I set them once and every agent respects them.

→ The card shows the exact payload before anything happens, not a vague "send emails?" prompt. For this run it was 240 recipients, 3 emails over 6 days, capped at 25 a day, first send at 8am, agent SPECTER.

→ I get three moves: approve and it runs, edit the payload then approve, or reject with a note and Claude takes the reason and replans from there.

→ When I approve, the turn resumes exactly where it paused. It does not restart the work or re-run the earlier steps, it continues from the same point, and the whole decision is written to the database with my user id and the timestamp.

- - -

The point is not that the AI is slow. The point is that control and speed stop being a trade-off. The agent does the work, you hold the gate, and every action is tied back to the person who approved it. When you add a teammate later, that same trail is how you trust them with the agents too. That is the difference between a demo you watch and a system you run your company on.
