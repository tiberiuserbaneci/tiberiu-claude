RULE 01 · CORE
01
AI drafts. Humans approve. Always.
No agent sends without HUMAN_GATE. Not at 2am. Not at scale. Not when you're tired. The gate is the product. Skip it and you ship spam, burn the domain, and torch the brand on the same day.

02SIGNAL
No signal, no send.
SPECTER scans first. If there's no trigger (raise, hire, launch, churn), the draft never gets written. Spray-and-pray is over.
03VOICE
1-of-1, not 1-of-1000.
Every message names one person, one company, one reason. If a sentence reads the same for 50 prospects, STRIKER rewrites it.
04DOMAIN
Never send from the brand domain.
Outbound runs on a burner. The brand domain stays clean for reply, contract, and invoice. One blast on the main domain ends Google deliverability.
05DECAY
Signal has a half-life. Send today.
A raise is hot for 48h. By day 7 it's noise. PULSE times the follow-up. Late beats never, but on-time beats both.
06MEMORY
Every verdict feeds the next draft.
GATE approves, edits, or kills. All three feed CORTEX. Week 12 reply rate is 9x week 1 because the system remembers.
07ROUTING
Use the right model for the job.
Gemini for scoring, Claude for tone, GPT for structure. One model for everything is a 2024 mindset. Routing is the moat.
08AUDIT
Log every send. Forever.
SENTINEL writes the diary. Who, why, what came back. If you can't audit the agent, you don't own the agent.
09TIME
If it costs more than 15 min/day, redesign.
Operator time is the only metric that matters. 1,847 ops / 12 min is the bar. Anything above 15 means the system is wrong.
SIGNED
9 rules. 0 exceptions. If the agent breaks one, the agent gets fixed.
