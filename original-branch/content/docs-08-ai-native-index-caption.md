## CAPTION

Fewer than 1 in 100 companies are actually built for the AI era. An independent index scored 58,351 of them to prove it.

- - -

I wanted an honest answer to who is really AI-native, not who says they are. So we scored 58,351 companies from 0 to 100 across six dimensions, Moat, Ops, Proof, Discoverability, Build and Product, and indexed $1.8T of annual revenue along the way. The result was blunt. Most companies have adopted AI as a tool and stopped there. Almost none have rebuilt around it.

- - -

→ Only 0.7%, that is 408 companies, scored truly AI-native. The other 99.3% are Absent, Aware, Adopting or Enabled.

→ Half the market, 49%, is stuck at Aware or Adopting. They use AI, but it does not run anything.

→ The three weakest dimensions across the whole set are Moat at 47, Ops at 52 and Proof at 52 out of 100.

→ Those three are the wedge. A proprietary data flywheel, operations run by agents, and proof generated on autopilot are exactly what most companies are missing.

→ The gap is not awareness. It is structure. AI-native is an architecture, not a feature you bolt on.

- - -

The weakest dimensions are not a problem, they are the opportunity. The companies that close Moat, Ops and Proof first are the ones that pull away, because everyone else is still treating AI like a faster autocomplete.
