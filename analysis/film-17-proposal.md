# Film 17 - built as a run-board model

Operator sign-off: subject and script approved 2026-08-07.

Ninth on the run-board model. The subject is the founder as the content bottleneck:
forty-seven half-written drafts, nothing published. The keyword DRAFT is free.

---

## Subject

**The post that never got finished.** A founder at 2 to 10 people knows they should be
creating content, but every draft sits in a folder with a timestamp from three weeks ago.
The content calendar is a spreadsheet with green cells showing what was planned and red
cells showing what never happened.

- **Keyword** DRAFT. Free, not in CLAUDE.md 6 or any shipped film.
- **Collision** Clean.
- **ICP** Direct. Every founder who has been told "you need to post more" and has a
  notes app full of half-thoughts.

The story is not "we write your content." It is "the bottleneck is not ideas. The
bottleneck is the founder finding the time to finish one."

---

## Numbers

Same universe. One week, four half-finished pieces, one that went out.

---

## Script

**Hook**

    *Claude* finished|the post I had|been writing|for *three* weeks.

**Beat 1** - Claude finished the post I had been writing for three weeks. It took one
week and four drafts I had already abandoned.

**Beat 2** - Monday. It reads my scattered notes and builds the structure I could not
see, so the draft has a spine instead of a list of thoughts.

**Beat 3** - Wednesday. It writes the opening from my own voice, not a template, so the
post sounds like me on a good day rather than me at midnight.

**Beat 4** - Thursday. It cuts the part where I try to explain everything at once, and
keeps the one point that matters.

**Beat 5** - Friday. The post goes out. Not because I found three hours. Because the
draft was ready when I had ten minutes to check it.

**Beat 6** - Comment DRAFT and I will send you the exact run.

**Accent words:** finished, three, weeks, abandoned, spine, voice, cuts, ready.

**Product line:** This is the Ultron platform, one input and a team that runs behind it.

---

## Objects

| Beat | Object |
|---|---|
| 2 | `notes` | scattered fragments into structured outline |
| 3 | `voice` | opening paragraph in founder's tone |
| 4 | `blade` | the cut: before and after the edit |
| 5 | `post` | published piece with timestamp |
| 6 | `stamp` | the ask |

Head object: a stack of paper — scattered notes, one sheet lifted and highlighted.
Not dial, stack, fan, doc, profile, receipt, message, gap, signal, response, pipeline bar.

---

## What this film may not say

- No claim that the system generates ideas. It structures existing notes.
- No claim that it posts without review. Founder checks and publishes.
- No claim that it replaces a writer. It finishes what the founder started.
