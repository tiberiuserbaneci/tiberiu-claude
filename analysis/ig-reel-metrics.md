# IG reel metrics, per post

The operator's own numbers, entered as he sends them. CLAUDE.md 29 makes this loop binding: read
what shipped before building the next one. Rates are computed on **reach**, not views, because
reach is people and views double count rewatches.

## 01 · 6 APPS TO RUN YOUR LIFE (like a real CEO)

Reel, 7s, static card. Posted **Sat 15 Aug, 1:34pm**. Caption CTA: "Comment CEO and I'll send you
the direct links". **Tested WINNER on reach.**

| Raw | | Computed on reach (1,978) | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Views | 2,912 | Views / reach | **1.47** (rewatched) |
| Reach | 1,978 | Saves / reach | **3.99%** |
| Interactions | 125 | Likes / reach | 1.62% |
| Saves | 79 | Shares / reach | 0.51% |
| Likes | 32 | Comments / reach | **0.10%** |
| Shares | 10 | Follows / reach | **0.15%** |
| Comments | 2 | Saves / interactions | **63%** |
| Follows | 3 | Saves : likes | **2.5 : 1** |
| Hook rate | 52.5% | Avg watch (7s x hold) | ~2.2s |
| Hold rate | 31.3% | | |

Retention decays close to linearly from second one: 100 -> ~65 at 0:02 -> ~50 at 0:03 -> ~35 at
0:05 -> ~17 at the end. No cliff, which means nothing specific drove people off; a static card
simply has nothing left to hold them once it is read.

### What this proves

**The card format is a save machine.** 3.99% of everyone reached saved it, against the 0.45 to
0.90% band CLAUDE.md 26 recorded as good. Saves are 63% of all interactions and outnumber likes
2.5 to 1, which is the signature of a reference asset rather than a piece of entertainment. The
1.47 views per person says they scrolled back to read the list. The layout, the logos and the one
line per tool are doing their job and should not be touched on this evidence.

### What this disproves, and it is my error to own

**A complete card kills the comment CTA.** 79 people saved it and 2 commented. The caption offered
"the direct links", but the frame already named all six tools, so the reader had no reason to ask
for anything: they can find six product names themselves in seconds. CLAUDE.md 26 recorded keyword
CTAs pulling 23 to 34 comments; this pulled 2. The lever is not a better CTA line, it is
**withholding**: show five of six and put the sixth in the DM, or show all six names and hold back
the how (the prompt, the order, the settings). **The DM must contain something the frame cannot.**

**Follows are near zero: 3 from 1,978.** The material was useful and promised nothing next. "Follow
for more AI tools and productivity hacks" does not name what is coming, so there is no reason to
subscribe rather than just save. Worth testing a footer that names the series or the next post.

### Open, not yet answerable on one post

- Saturday 1:34pm against CLAUDE.md 22's 10 to 11am weekday. One data point cannot separate the
  slot from the hook.
- Whether 52.5% hook rate is strong for this account. Needs the losers' hook rates to compare.
- Whether the near-linear decay is the format's ceiling or the length's. A 7s static card may
  simply be read in 2.2s, in which case hold rate is measuring completeness, not boredom.

| Reach | 1,331 | Saves / reach | **3.46%** |
| Interactions | 86 | Likes / reach | 2.40% |
| Saves | 46 | Shares / reach | 0.53% |
| Likes | 32 | Comments / reach | **0.08%** |
| Shares | 7 | Follows / reach | 0.23% |
| Comments | 1 | Saves / interactions | 53.5% |
| Follows | 3 | Saves : likes | 1.44 : 1 |
| Hook rate | **40.6%** | Avg watch time | 0:06 of 0:07 |
| Hold rate | 30.8% | | |

Note the apparent conflict between hold rate 30.8% and average watch 6s of 7s. They are not the
same measure: hold is most likely the share who reached the end, while average watch is inflated by
loops. Not resolvable from the screenshot, so nothing is concluded from it.

---

## What the two together establish

**THE HOOK OWNS REACH, AND HOOK RATE IS THE CLEAN COMPARISON.** 52.5% against 40.6%, and reach
followed it down by a third. Hook rate is a rate *among those served*, so unlike reach it is not a
function of how widely the algorithm chose to push the post: the 12 point gap is about the words,
not the luck. The two posts also went out on different days and times (Sat 1:34pm, Sun 11:53am),
which is a real confound for reach but much less so for hook rate.

**"YOUR LIFE" AND AN IDENTITY BEAT A BUSINESS VERB.** `RUN YOUR LIFE (like a real CEO)` carries an
identity the viewer wants and a subject everybody has. `SUPERCHARGE YOUR BUSINESS` leads with a
marketing verb, and "supercharge" is the single word most likely to read as an advert. It also asks
the viewer to think of themselves as a business owner in the first half second, which is a narrower
door than "your life".

**THE FORMAT OWNS SAVES, NOT THE HOOK.** 3.99% and 3.46% of reach saved. Two different hooks, two
different reach levels, the same very high save rate. So the card, the logos and the one line per
tool are what produce saves, and the hook only changes how many people arrive to save it. **Two
separate levers: write the hook for reach, build the card for saves.**

**COMMENTS ARE BROKEN BY THE FORMAT, NOT BY THE KEYWORD.** CEO pulled 2, TOOLS pulled 1. The second
CTA was the better written of the two ("the full 7-tool pyramid stack" promises structure, not just
links) and still did worse. A complete card leaves nothing to ask for. Confirmed on two posts now,
so the fix is structural: **withhold one row, or withhold the how.**

**FOLLOWS ARE FLAT AT 3.** Identical on both, 0.15% and 0.23% of reach. Nothing in the current
format gives a reason to subscribe rather than save and move on.

**LIKES ARE FLAT AT 32.** Identical on both despite a 33% reach gap, which suggests 32 is close to
the account's committed core rather than a function of this post.
