# Film 18 - built on the run-board model

Operator sign-off: subject and script approved 2026-08-07.

Tenth on the argument model. The subject is the follow-up that should have been sent but
was not: the nudge that keeps deals alive. The keyword NUDGE is free.

---

## Subject

**The deal that died because nobody nudged it.** A founder at 2 to 10 people closes
like they sell: with conviction and then silence. The follow-up is the exact thing that
never happens, because the founder is already in the next call.

- **Keyword** NUDGE. Free, not in CLAUDE.md 6 or any shipped film.
- **Collision** Clean.
- **ICP** Direct. Every founder who knows they should follow up and never does.

The story is not "we send follow-ups for you." It is "the nudge is the difference
between a deal that closes and a deal that ghosts, and the founder is always the one
who was supposed to send it."

---

## Numbers

Same universe. One deal, four moments where a nudge would have moved it, one that did.

---

## Script

**Hook**

    *Claude* sent|the nudge I|kept *promising*|I would send.

**Beat 1** - Claude sent the nudge I kept promising I would send. It took one deal and
four moments where I meant to follow up and never did.

**Beat 2** - Day two. It drafts the check-in from the call notes, in my own words, so
I do not sound like a CRM sequence.

**Beat 3** - Day five. It flags the silence: no reply, no open, no click, and drafts
the next thing to say.

**Beat 4** - Day ten. It writes the re-activation from the last conversation, not from
the template.

**Beat 5** - Day fourteen. The reply arrives. Not because the product changed. Because
the nudge reached them at the right moment.

**Beat 6** - Comment NUDGE and I will send you the exact run.

**Accent words:** nudge, promising, check-in, silence, re-activation, moment, reply, right.

**Product line:** This is the Ultron platform, one input and a team that runs behind it.

## What this film may not say

- No claim that it sends without review. Founder checks and sends.
- No claim that it replaces relationship building. It drafts from context.
- No claim that every nudge gets a reply. It sends the right one at the right time.
