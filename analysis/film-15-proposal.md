## Why this film is not built from film 12

Films 10, 11 and 12 are the same shape: a named window of time, split into numbered rows,
one action per row. That shape has now run three times. CLAUDE.md 30 blocks a fourth on the
same opening object and the operator's word was "un altul nou creat de la zero".

There is also a new reason, and it is the real one. The clay objects in every shipped film are
`box-shadow` stacks - N offset copies of a rectangle, with no light in them and no material.
They cannot turn, because there is nothing there to turn. As of today the objects are real
geometry with a studio environment, a moving key light and a cast shadow, which means **one
object can carry a whole film by changing** instead of six objects appearing in sequence.

That is the format this proposal is for. It is not the run board with a nicer texture.

## Subject

**The pipeline review that never happens.**

A founder's pipeline shows a number. The founder knows the number is soft and reviews it
anyway, or more often does not, because a real review costs an afternoon they do not have.
The film is the review, run properly, once.

| Check | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Collision** | Clean. 10 was ONE stalled deal over seven days. 11 was the inbound hour. 12 was one contract. 05 was cold outbound. None of them is the whole pipeline at once, and none of them is a judgement call about what is real. |
| **ICP** | Direct. At 2 to 50 people the founder IS the pipeline. Nobody else is going to tell them which deals are fiction. |
| **Product mapping** | Real, not rhetoric. Qualification, discovery and objection handling are shipped surfaces. The film shows the judgement, not a feature list. |
| **Whose clock** | The FOUNDER's, per the rule film 12 paid for. The number in the film is **three weeks of putting it off**, not how many seconds the machine took. The machine's speed is never named. |
| **Keyword** | `PIPELINE`. Checked against CLAUDE.md 6, the REALNUMBERS set, all twelve shipped film keywords, all twelve carousel keywords, and the parallel session's WEEK and SEATS. Free. `FUNNEL` is taken by script 06 and is a different thing. |

## Beats and the object run

Six beats. The object changes at 3 and again at 5, so no object carries beat 2 and beat 4
(CLAUDE.md 30, graphic variety).

| Beat | Object | What it does | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **RING**, full, reading 41 | head object. Real torus, recessed face, key light swings across it | hook + "It had been three weeks since I last looked at it honestly." |
| 2 | **RING turns** | the face re-reads as it rotates. This is the shot no previous film could take. | "It read every note, every thread, every date I had not opened." |
| 3 | **COLUMN of slabs** | the 41 deals as real extruded rows, lit from the same key | "Twenty two had not moved in a month and nobody had said no." |
| 4 | **COLUMN cuts** | rows drop out of the light, one group at a time, casting real shadows as they go | "Ten were a person who liked me and could not sign anything." |
| 5 | **ONE SLAB lifted** | a single row rises out of the column into the key light | "Nine were real. One of them was closer than everything I had above it." |
| 6 | **CTA** | COMMENT as display type, per the standing note | "Comment PIPELINE and I will send you the exact run." |

The honesty limit, held on screen and in the caption, same as 08 and 12 held theirs:
**it ranks and it argues, the founder decides and the founder sends.** No deal is closed by
the film and nothing is forecast.
