## Subject

**The competitor you found out about from a customer.** Not from research, not from a
network post, not from a funding announcement. From one customer who mentioned them in
passing during a call. The competitor that wins by word of mouth is the exact one Google
search will never surface.

- **Keyword** FIELD. Free, checked against CLAUDE.md 6 table and every shipped film keyword.
- **Collision** Clean. FIELD is not in any shipped film, script, or lead-magnet list.
- **ICP** Direct. A founder at 2 to 10 people does not have a competitive intelligence
  team. They discover competitors reactively, when a customer mentions them.

The story is not "we track every competitor." It is "the one competitor that matters most
is the one winning by word of mouth, because that is the signal you will never find until
a customer says it out loud."

---

## Numbers

Same universe as the shipped films. One customer call, one competitor mentioned, one
positioning gap the founder did not know they had.

Nothing here claims a specific competitor name, a market share figure, or a win rate. The
outcome the film claims is the one the product performs: it flags competitor mentions from
customer conversations and drafts the competitive response.

## Script

**Hook** (four lines)

    *Claude* tracked|the competitor|I only found|because a *customer* named them.

**Beat 1** - Claude tracked the competitor I only found because a customer named them.
One call and my entire competitive map changed.

**Beat 2** - What the customer described: a feature I had never seen, because I had been
building what I thought they wanted.

**Beat 3** - My research never found them because they do not market. They win by word of
mouth, which is the exact signal that never shows up on a search.

**Beat 4** - The system flags these mentions from every customer conversation, before they
become the reason you lose the next deal.

**Beat 5** - It drafts the response in my own words: what we do, what they do, and where
the line is.

**Beat 6** - Comment FIELD and I will send you the exact run.

**Accent words:** customer, competitor, feature, research, word, flags, response, line.

**Product line (caption, not VO):** This is the Ultron platform, one input and a team that
runs behind it.
