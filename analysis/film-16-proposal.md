# Film 16 - built as a single-moment argument

Operator sign-off: subject and script approved 2026-08-07.

Eighth on the run-board model, built from episode 13 so the chain carries, because a
pipeline is a run: it has stages and elapsed time between them. The subject is pipeline
visibility without a CRM. The keyword PIPELINE is free.

---

## Subject

**The deal that died because nobody looked at the pipeline.** A founder at 2 to 10 people
does not have a CRM. They track deals in a spreadsheet they opened once and never updated.
The deal that needs attention today is three rows down in a tab they have not clicked in
two weeks.

- **Keyword** PIPELINE. Free, checked against CLAUDE.md 6 and every shipped film.
- **Collision** Clean. No shipped film covers pipeline or CRM visibility.
- **ICP** Direct. A founder who closes deals themselves and has no sales operations
  function updating a board.

The story is not "our pipeline is better." It is "the pipeline only works if someone
updates it, and the founder is the only person who can, and they do not have time."

---

## Numbers

Same universe. Four deals, one that moved backward without anyone noticing.

---

## Script

**Hook**

    *Claude* showed me|the deal that|was going backward|before I *noticed*.

**Beat 1** - Claude showed me the deal that was going backward before I noticed. It took
one pipeline and four deals I thought were fine.

**Beat 2** - Monday. It pulls the stages from every conversation, so the pipeline is
always current even when I forget to update it.

**Beat 3** - Wednesday. It flags the deal that has not moved in ten days, not because it
is dead but because nobody has checked.

**Beat 4** - Thursday. It drafts the follow-up from the stage, in my own words, so I do
not sound like I only remembered because the spreadsheet told me to.

**Beat 5** - Friday. The week-end summary arrives with what moved, what stalled, and
what I close next week.

**Beat 6** - Comment PIPELINE and I will send you the exact run.

**Accent words:** deal, backward, pipeline, moved, stalled, follow-up, close, next.

**Product line:** This is the Ultron platform, one input and a team that runs behind it.

## What this film may not say

- No claim that the pipeline updates itself for every tool. It pulls from conversations.
- No claim that it replaces a CRM. It makes the one you have visible.
- Nothing sends itself. Founder reviews and sends.
