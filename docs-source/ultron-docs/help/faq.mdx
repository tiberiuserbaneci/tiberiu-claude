<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long do tasks take?">
    Most tasks complete in **2-5 minutes**. Simple content tasks (Pulse writing a LinkedIn post) are on the faster end. Multi-step research tasks (Cortex auditing a competitor and finding 10 leads) take longer. You can watch progress in real time in the Command Center activity feed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I run multiple agents at once?">
    Yes. Assign different tasks to different agents — they run independently and in parallel. Tell Specter to find leads, tell Pulse to write content, tell Cortex to audit a competitor. All three run simultaneously. Results land in their respective sections of your dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do agents have access to my email?">
    Only if you explicitly connect Gmail through the Google Workspace integration — and only for tasks you assign. Striker reads your inbox when you tell it to triage or find deal opportunities. It does not read your email proactively without a task.

    You can disconnect Gmail at any time from Integrations to revoke access immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data private?">
    Yes. Each workspace is fully isolated. Agents only see your leads, your content, and your profile data. No agent from another user's workspace can access your data. Your data is never used to train or improve models for other users.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I undo what an agent does?">
    Agents **draft content** and **save leads** — they don't publish or send anything automatically. Every piece of content Pulse creates lands in Content Drops as a draft. Every lead Specter finds goes into your Leads table with status "new." Nothing goes live or sends without your explicit approval.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if an agent makes a mistake?">
    Agents log every action in the activity feed — you can see exactly what they did and what data they used. If a lead is wrong, delete it. If a content draft is off, request a revision. Agents also save what works to memory, so if they make the same mistake twice, you can delete that memory entry to prevent it from recurring.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I customize agent behavior?">
    Yes, through your profile settings. Voice and tone settings directly affect Pulse's output. Your ICP description shapes how Specter scores and targets leads. Your competitor list affects Cortex's research angles. Your case studies and objections affect how Striker writes proposals and handles deals.

    The more complete your profile, the more customized your agents behave.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What AI models power the agents?">
    Agents run on enterprise-grade AI models via your connected API key (Claude or OpenRouter). The specific model used is determined by your integration choice — Claude API uses Anthropic's Claude models, OpenRouter gives you access to multiple providers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there an API?">
    An API for power users and developers is on the roadmap. If you need early access or want to be notified, contact us at hello@51ultron.com.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I white-label Ultron for clients?">
    Yes, through the Custom plan. We work with agencies and resellers who want to offer Ultron-powered services under their own brand. Contact us to discuss what white-label looks like for your use case.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Free and Max?">
    On Free, agents operate in preview mode — they confirm what they'd do but don't fully execute. On Max, they execute completely: leads land in your table, content appears in Content Drops, inbox gets triaged. Free gives you 5 conversations per day. Max is unlimited. $19/month for Max.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I pay for AI usage on top of the $19/month?">
    Yes, separately. The $19/month covers the platform. AI inference costs go directly to your provider (Anthropic or OpenRouter via your own API key). Typical AI spend for an active user is $15-50/month. Ultron never marks up or charges for AI calls.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel?">
    Your data stays in your workspace. You can export leads, content, and deals at any time from your dashboard. If you downgrade to Free, you keep access to view and export everything — you just can't run new full-execution tasks.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
