---
title: "ICP Deep Match"
description: "Score a list of companies against your ICP definition and rank them for outreach priority."
icon: "bullseye-arrow"
---

ICP Deep Match takes a list of companies (or criteria to find them) and scores each against your exact ICP definition from your profile. The output is a prioritized list — highest-fit companies at the top with a clear rationale for each score.

## What it produces

```text
ICP MATCH RESULTS — 20 companies evaluated

Score 9/10 — Acme Corp
  Size: 180 employees (target: 100–500) ✓
  Stage: Series B (target: Series A–B) ✓
  Industry: B2B SaaS (target: B2B SaaS) ✓
  Geography: US (target: US/Canada) ✓
  Signal: VP Sales hire + enterprise tier launch ✓
  Gap: Tech stack unconfirmed

Score 7/10 — Beta Inc
  Size: 45 employees (below target range)
  Stage: Series A ✓
  Industry: B2B SaaS ✓
  Signal: No recent triggers
  Recommendation: Warm nurture

Score 4/10 — Gamma Co
  Size: 2,000 employees (above target)
  Stage: Public
  Recommendation: Archive
```

## Configuration

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Model | Kimi K2 |
| Max turns | 8 |
| Execution | Fork |
| Tools | `web_search`, `search_companies`, `get_company_profile`, `search_memories`, `save_lead` |

## When to use it

- Cleaning an existing lead list before running outreach
- Prioritizing a large prospect list when you can't contact everyone
- Validating whether a new market segment fits your ICP before investing in research

## How to invoke

```text
Score these 20 companies against my ICP: [list]
```

```text
Find 30 companies in the HR tech space and score them against my ICP.
```
