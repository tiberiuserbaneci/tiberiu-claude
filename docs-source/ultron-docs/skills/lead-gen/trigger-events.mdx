The best time to reach a prospect is right after something changes. Trigger Events finds companies where something just changed — and surfaces the right outreach angle for each one.

## Triggers Ultron watches

| Trigger | Why it matters | Outreach angle |
|---------|---------------|----------------|
| Funding round closed | Company has budget, is actively scaling | "Congrats on the raise — most teams at your stage hit [X] next" |
| New executive hire | Evaluation cycle in motion, new priorities | Reference the hire, address their mandate |
| Job posting pattern | Hiring X roles signals specific problems | "You're hiring 3 SDRs — here's what most teams get wrong at that stage" |
| Competitor switch | Highest-intent signal available | "Saw you moved off [competitor] — here's what teams usually need next" |
| News / press coverage | Raised profile, ego is up, receptive | Reference the press, acknowledge the milestone |
| Tech stack change | Infrastructure decision signals adjacent needs | Address the new stack specifically |

## What it produces

```text
TRIGGER EVENTS — this week

Acme Corp — Series B announced April 3
  Signal strength: High
  Contact: Sarah Chen (CEO)
  Angle: "Congrats on the $42M raise. Most B2B teams at your stage
          hit the ops wall right after Series B. Worth 20 minutes?"

Beta Inc — New VP Sales hired from Workato (April 1)
  Signal strength: High
  Contact: New VP (Rachel Kim)
  Angle: "Saw you just joined Beta as VP Sales. Teams coming from
          Workato usually have specific expectations about integrations..."

Gamma Co — 3 SDR job postings (this week)
  Signal strength: Medium
  Contact: Head of Sales
  Angle: "You're scaling the SDR team. Here's the playbook most teams
          wish they had before the first hire."
```
