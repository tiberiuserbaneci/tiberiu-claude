Two skills that bracket every important sales conversation: a pre-call brief you generate before the meeting, and a post-call blueprint you generate after.

## Pre-call brief

Generated in under 3 minutes from a name and company. Contains:

- Company snapshot (size, funding, product, recent news)
- Who you're meeting (background, past companies, public takes)
- Why now (current signals and triggers)
- Recommended talking points (3–5, specific to their situation)
- Anticipated objections and counters
- Questions to ask

Saved to memory automatically — Striker can reference it when writing the post-call follow-up.

**How to invoke:**
```text
Prep me for my call with the CTO at Linear tomorrow.
```

## Post-call blueprint

Generated after the meeting from your call notes or a transcript. Produces a full canvas with:

- **Meeting recap** — key points discussed, decisions made
- **Audience profile** — updated picture of who you're selling to based on the call
- **Content that resonates** — topics and angles they responded to
- **Outreach plan** — next steps, timing, who owns what
- **Competitor mentions** — what they said about the competition
- **Sales playbook update** — what to do differently next time
- **Outcomes** — where the deal stands, next milestone
- **Follow-up email draft** — ready to send, references the specific conversation

**How to invoke:**
```text
Create a post-call blueprint. Here are my notes: [paste notes]
```

```text
I just finished a call with Acme Corp. The CTO mentioned budget concerns and
asked about our enterprise tier. They want a proposal by Friday. Create the blueprint.
```
