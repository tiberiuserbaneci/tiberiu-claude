The meeting prep skill generates a full pre-call brief in under 3 minutes. Everything you need to walk into a conversation prepared — without spending an hour on LinkedIn and Google.

## What it produces

```text
MEETING PREP — Sarah Chen, CEO @ Acme Corp

Company snapshot
  Acme Corp | Series B ($42M) | 180 employees | SF
  Product: B2B workflow automation for operations teams
  Recent: Launched enterprise tier Q1 2026, hired VP Sales from Workato

About Sarah Chen
  CEO since founding (2019) | ex-Salesforce (VP Product), ex-McKinsey
  Public takes: advocates for ops-led growth, skeptical of AI hype without ROI
  Recent post: "The ops tools market is broken. We're fixing it."

Why now
  - New enterprise tier means new complexity, new needs
  - VP Sales hire signals they're scaling the revenue motion
  - 3 open SDR roles = actively investing in outbound

Talking points
  1. The enterprise launch — what new challenges came with it?
  2. The Workato VP hire — how is that changing how they think about integrations?
  3. The SDR investment — what does the outbound playbook look like at their scale?

Anticipated objections
  "We already have a solution" — ask what's working and what isn't at enterprise scale
  "Not the right time" — their SDR hiring says the opposite; they're moving now

Questions to ask
  1. What's the biggest bottleneck in your ops workflow since launching enterprise?
  2. How are you thinking about AI in the stack right now?
  3. What would make the next 90 days a success for your team?
```

## Configuration

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Model | Kimi K2 |
| Max turns | 8 |
| Execution | Fork |
| Tools | `web_search`, `scrape_website`, `search_people`, `search_memories`, `get_company_profile`, `save_memory` |

## How to invoke

```text
Meeting prep for my call with the CTO at Linear tomorrow at 2pm.
```

```text
I have a call with Sarah Chen at Acme Corp on Friday. Prep me.
```

Results are saved to memory automatically — Striker can reference the brief when writing the follow-up email after the call.
