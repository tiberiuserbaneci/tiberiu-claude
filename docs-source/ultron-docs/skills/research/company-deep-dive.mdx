The company deep-dive skill produces a structured brief on any company in minutes. Everything you need to understand the business before a meeting, an email, or a strategic decision.

## What it produces

- **Company overview** — what they build, who they sell to, founding story
- **Funding history** — round sizes, investors, total raised, implied valuation signals
- **Leadership** — CEO, CTO, VP Sales — backgrounds, past companies, notable quotes
- **Product** — core capabilities, recent launches, known gaps
- **Tech stack signals** — job postings and integrations that reveal infrastructure choices
- **Recent news** — press, product announcements, executive changes in the last 90 days
- **ICP fit score** — 1–10 against your defined ICP, with scoring rationale
- **Talking points** — 3–5 conversation angles based on their current situation
- **Anticipated objections** — likely pushback and suggested counters

## When to use it

- Before any important meeting
- Before adding a company to your outreach list
- When a company enters your pipeline and you need full context fast
- Before a proposal or pricing conversation

## How to invoke

```text
Deep dive on Notion — I'm pitching their CTO next Thursday.
```

```text
Give me a full company profile on Vercel.
```
