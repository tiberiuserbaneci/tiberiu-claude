The pricing research skill maps pricing across your competitive landscape. Not estimates — actual pricing from live public pages, with tier breakdowns and observed patterns.

## What it produces

- **Tier breakdown per competitor** — plan names, prices, what's included per tier
- **Pricing model analysis** — per-seat vs usage-based vs flat vs freemium
- **Market patterns** — where the market clusters, what's above and below average
- **Positioning observations** — who prices on value vs who races to the bottom
- **Gaps and opportunities** — price points or packaging patterns no one is using

## Configuration

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Model | Kimi K2 |
| Max turns | 8 |
| Execution | Fork |
| Tools | `web_search`, `web_search_multiple`, `scrape_website`, `browser_action`, `search_memories` |

## Anti-fabrication rule

Only pricing found on live public pages is reported as fact. Gated pricing pages are flagged: "Enterprise pricing not listed publicly — contact sales noted on pricing page." No estimates or interpolations.

## How to invoke

```text
Research pricing across CRM tools in the mid-market B2B space.
```

```text
Map pricing for all major sales engagement platforms — I need to know where to position our new enterprise tier.
```
