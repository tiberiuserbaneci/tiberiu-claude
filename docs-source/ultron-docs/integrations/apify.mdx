---
title: "Apify"
description: "LinkedIn scraping, social media data, and web extraction for Cortex and Specter."
---

Apify gives Cortex and Specter the ability to scrape live social media data — LinkedIn posts and profiles, Twitter threads, Instagram content, and structured data from any website. It's what powers intent signal detection and social-proof prospecting.

## What it enables

- LinkedIn profile scraping — find contacts matching specific criteria
- LinkedIn post scraping — find who's posting about your target pain points
- Twitter/X thread scraping — identify trending conversations in your space
- Instagram content data — useful if your audience is on Instagram
- Structured data extraction from any website

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Apify API token">
    1. Log in to [apify.com](https://apify.com)
    2. Go to your **Account → Integrations → API**
    3. Copy your API token
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to Ultron">
    1. Open your Ultron dashboard → **Integrations**
    2. Find the Apify card and click **Connect**
    3. Paste your API token and save
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which agents use Apify

| Agent | How Apify helps |
|-------|----------------|
| **Cortex** | Scrapes competitor sites and social content for research |
| **Specter** | Finds prospects via LinkedIn activity and intent signals |
| **Pulse** | Scrapes trending content patterns for content strategy |

## Without Apify

Cortex and Specter use web search as a fallback. You lose access to deep LinkedIn scraping and social intent signals, which limits the quality of pain-point targeting prompts like _"find founders posting about [problem] this week."_
