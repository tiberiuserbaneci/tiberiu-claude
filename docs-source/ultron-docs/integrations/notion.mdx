title: "Notion"
description: "Sync research reports and content drafts to your Notion workspace."
---

The Notion integration lets agents push outputs directly to your Notion workspace. Research reports from Cortex, content drafts from Pulse, and competitor intelligence from Sentinel can all land in Notion pages automatically.

## What it enables

- Research reports synced to a Notion database of your choice
- Content drafts saved to your Notion content calendar
- Agent activity summaries pushed to Notion pages
- Competitor intelligence reports saved to your Notion knowledge base

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Notion integration">
    1. Go to [notion.so/my-integrations](https://notion.so/my-integrations)
    2. Click **New integration**
    3. Name it "Ultron" and select your workspace
    4. Under capabilities, enable: **Read content**, **Update content**, **Insert content**
    5. Click **Submit** and copy the **Internal Integration Token**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share pages with the integration">
    For each Notion page or database you want Ultron to write to:
    1. Open the page in Notion
    2. Click **...** → **Add connections**
    3. Search for "Ultron" and connect it

    Agents can only write to pages you've explicitly shared with the integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to Ultron">
    1. Open your Ultron dashboard → **Integrations**
    2. Find the Notion card and click **Connect**
    3. Paste your integration token and save
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Telling agents to use Notion

Once connected, you can tell agents where to save outputs:

```
Research [competitor] and save the report to my Notion knowledge base
```
```
Write a week of LinkedIn posts and save them to my Notion content calendar
```

If you don't specify Notion, outputs default to Content Drops and the activity log in your dashboard.
