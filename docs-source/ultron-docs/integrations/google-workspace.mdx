---

Google Workspace is the most impactful integration for sales. Once connected, Striker can read your inbox, identify deal opportunities, draft context-aware replies, and schedule follow-ups on your calendar — from your actual email address.

## What it enables

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Gmail" icon="envelope">
    - Inbox triage — Striker flags deal-related emails with suggested next steps
    - Context-aware follow-ups — Striker reads your email history before drafting
    - Outreach sending — send emails drafted by agents from your real Gmail address
    - Reply drafting — never write a "just checking in" email again
  </Card>
  <Card title="Google Calendar" icon="calendar">
    - Schedule follow-up meetings directly from a task
    - Block prep time before demos automatically
    - Check availability before suggesting meeting times in emails
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Integrations">
    Open your dashboard and navigate to **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Google">
    Find the Google Workspace card and click **Connect Google**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the OAuth flow">
    A Google consent screen will open. Sign in with the Google account you want to connect — this should be the email address you use for outreach.

    Grant the requested permissions for Gmail and Calendar access.

    <Warning>
      Use your primary business email. Agents will read from and draft to this address. If you connect a secondary account, outreach will come from that address.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm connection">
    Once authorized, you'll be redirected back to Integrations. The Google card will show as **Connected** with your email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What agents can do with Gmail

| Action | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Read inbox** | Striker can see recent emails to identify deal opportunities |
| **Read thread history** | Striker reads previous conversations before drafting replies |
| **Draft emails** | Striker creates drafts in your Gmail drafts folder |
| **Send emails** | When you explicitly ask, Striker can send from your address |

<Warning>
  Agents **never send emails automatically**. Striker will draft and save, but sending requires explicit instruction from you. You're always in control.
</Warning>

## Permissions granted

When you connect Google, you authorize access to:

- Gmail (read and send)
- Google Calendar (read and write)

You can revoke access at any time from [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) or by clicking **Disconnect** on the Integrations page.

## Disconnecting

Go to **Integrations**, find the Google Workspace card, and click **Disconnect**. Agent access to your Gmail and Calendar is revoked immediately. Any previously created drafts remain in your Gmail drafts folder.
