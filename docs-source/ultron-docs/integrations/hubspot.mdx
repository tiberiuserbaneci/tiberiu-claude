---
title: "HubSpot"
description: "Sync your leads and deals to HubSpot so Ultron and your CRM stay in sync."
---

HubSpot integration keeps your Ultron leads and deals synchronized with your CRM. Leads Specter finds flow into HubSpot contacts. Deals Striker tracks update in both places.

## What it enables

- Leads from Specter automatically sync to HubSpot contacts
- Deal stage updates in Ultron reflect in HubSpot deals
- Outreach activity logged in HubSpot contact timelines
- Pull existing HubSpot contacts into Ultron for research and enrichment

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a HubSpot private app">
    1. Log in to HubSpot → **Settings → Integrations → Private Apps**
    2. Click **Create a private app**
    3. Name it "Ultron"
    4. Under **Scopes**, enable:
       - `crm.objects.contacts.read`
       - `crm.objects.contacts.write`
       - `crm.objects.deals.read`
       - `crm.objects.deals.write`
    5. Click **Create app** and copy the access token
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to Ultron">
    1. Open your Ultron dashboard → **Integrations**
    2. Find the HubSpot card and click **Connect**
    3. Paste your HubSpot access token and save
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sync behavior

| Direction | What syncs |
|-----------|-----------|
| Ultron → HubSpot | New leads Specter finds, deal stage changes, outreach activity |
| HubSpot → Ultron | Existing contacts (for research and enrichment tasks) |

Sync happens when leads or deals are saved — not on a delay. When Specter saves a lead, it appears in HubSpot within seconds.
