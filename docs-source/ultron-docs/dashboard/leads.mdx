The Leads table is your prospect database. Every company or contact Specter finds gets saved here automatically — enriched with data, scored for fit, and ready for your next step.

## The leads table

Sortable by any column:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Name** | Contact's full name |
| **Title** | Job title |
| **Company** | Company name and domain |
| **Score** | Lead score 0–100 (ICP fit + intent signals) |
| **Status** | new → enriched → emailed → replied → meeting → closed / lost |
| **Source** | Which agent found them (usually Specter) |
| **Last Contact** | Date of most recent outreach |
| **Actions** | View profile, email, move to deal |

## Lead scores explained

Every lead is scored 0–100 when Specter saves it:

| Score | What it means |
|-------|--------------|
| **80–100** | Strong ICP fit, clear intent signals. Prioritize these. |
| **60–79** | Good fit, some signals. Worth pursuing. |
| **40–59** | Marginal fit. Nurture or deprioritize. |
| **0–39** | Doesn't match your ICP. Likely to skip. |

Score is based on: company size match, industry fit, hiring signals, funding stage, geographic match, and tech stack overlap. Open a lead profile to see exactly why it scored the way it did.

## Lead statuses

Move leads through these stages as you work them:

```
new → enriched → emailed → replied → meeting → closed / lost
```

- **new** — Specter just saved this lead. Not yet reviewed.
- **enriched** — additional data has been added (email verified, more context found)
- **emailed** — outreach has been sent
- **replied** — they responded to your outreach
- **meeting** — a call or meeting is scheduled
- **closed / lost** — deal outcome

You can move status manually, or tell Striker: _"Move all leads who replied this week to 'meeting' status."_

## Lead profile

Click any row to open the full lead profile slide-over:

- Contact info (name, title, email, LinkedIn URL)
- Company details (size, industry, location, funding stage)
- Lead score with full reasoning
- Research summary (what Specter or Cortex found about them)
- Outreach history (emails sent, replies received)
- Notes field for your own observations
- **"Move to deal"** button — promotes this lead into your Deals pipeline

## Filtering and searching

- Filter by score range (e.g., "show me only leads scored 70+")
- Filter by status (e.g., "show me all leads who haven't been emailed")
- Filter by source agent
- Filter by industry or location
- Search by name, company, or keyword in research notes

## Bulk actions

Select multiple leads with the checkbox column:

- **Bulk email** — tell Striker to draft outreach for all selected leads at once
- **Bulk status change** — move all selected to a new status
- **Export to CSV** — download for use in other tools
- **Move to deal** — bulk-promote high-score leads to your pipeline

## Where leads come from

| Source | How they get here |
|--------|-------------------|
| Specter | Direct lead gen tasks (most common) |
| Cortex | Research tasks that identify companies as leads |
| Manual | You can add leads directly with the "+ Add Lead" button |

<Tip>
  To prioritize: sort by Score descending, filter for Status = "new." That's your daily hit list — highest-fit leads who haven't been contacted yet.
</Tip>
