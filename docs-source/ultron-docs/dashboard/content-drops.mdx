Content Drops is where Pulse saves everything it writes. Posts, threads, emails, blog drafts, case studies — all organized and ready for your review. Nothing gets published without your approval.

## Views

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="List view">
    All content pieces in reverse chronological order. Columns:
    - **Title** — what the content is about
    - **Platform** — LinkedIn, Twitter, Blog, Email, General
    - **Status** — Draft, Approved, Scheduled, Published
    - **Agent** — which agent created it (usually Pulse, sometimes Striker for emails)
    - **Engagement prediction** — Low / Medium / High / Viral
    - **Created** — date and time
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Calendar view">
    Month view showing scheduled and published content by date. Drag and drop to reschedule. Click any piece to preview.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By platform">
    Filter to see only LinkedIn content, only email templates, only blog posts, etc.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Content statuses

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Draft** | Created by an agent, waiting for your review |
| **Approved** | You've reviewed it and it's ready to post |
| **Scheduled** | Set to publish at a specific date and time |
| **Published** | Marked as live on the platform |

<Note>
  Ultron doesn't publish directly to social platforms. You review and approve content here, then copy or schedule it using your platform of choice. Direct publishing integrations are on the roadmap.
</Note>

## Reviewing a content piece

Click any item to open the full preview:

- **Platform preview** — see how it will look formatted for the target platform (LinkedIn post formatting, Twitter thread numbering, etc.)
- **Edit inline** — make changes directly in the preview
- **Engagement prediction with reasoning** — Pulse explains why it predicted High or Viral
- **Alternative hooks** — 2-3 variations on the opening line if you want a different angle
- **Approve** — marks it ready to publish
- **Request revision** — type feedback and Pulse rewrites it

## Requesting a revision

If a content piece isn't right, open it and click "Request Revision." Type your specific feedback:

- _"Make the hook stronger — lead with the result, not the process"_
- _"Too formal — write it the way I talk, more casual"_
- _"Shorten it by 30% and cut the last section"_

Pulse will revise and drop the new version in the same thread.

## Bulk actions

Select multiple content pieces to:
- **Bulk approve** — move all to Approved status
- **Export** — download as a CSV or text file
- **Delete** — remove drafts you don't want

## What lands here automatically

Any task you assign to Pulse creates content in this view. You'll also see:
- Email drafts Striker creates for outreach campaigns
- Proposal drafts Striker generates for deals
- Research summaries Cortex formats as documents

Everything is in one place. No digging through chat history to find what the agent wrote.
