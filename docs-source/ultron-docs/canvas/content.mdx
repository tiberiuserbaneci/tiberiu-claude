Three canvas templates for content planning and creation.

## Content Calendar

Editorial calendar with platform, format, date, topic, hook, and status columns.

**What it shows:** A 1–4 week view with one row per piece of content. Each row: date, platform (LinkedIn / Twitter / Instagram / Blog), format (post / carousel / video / article), topic, hook draft, status (draft / scheduled / published).

**Populated from:** Your voice profile, platform preferences, and Pulse's trend research on your industry.

**How to trigger:**
```text
Create a 2-week content calendar for my B2B SaaS audience.
```

---

## Carousel

Multi-slide presentation format for LinkedIn or Instagram.

**Structure:** Slide 1 (hook, max 8 words) → Slides 2–8 (one point per slide, max 15 words each) → Final slide (CTA)

**Rendered as:** A styled slide deck inside the chat. Each slide is visible as a scrollable card.

**How to trigger:**
```text
Create a LinkedIn carousel on the 5 mistakes founders make when pricing their first SaaS product.
```

---

## Infographic

Visual narrative with stats, callouts, and logical flow.

**Sections:** Headline, 3–5 key stats with sources, narrative flow with icons, attribution

**Pre-research:** All statistics sourced from live web search. No invented numbers.

**How to trigger:**
```text
Create an infographic on the state of B2B outbound in 2026 using real data.
```
