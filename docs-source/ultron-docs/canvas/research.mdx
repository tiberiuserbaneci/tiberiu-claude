Five canvas templates for research output. Built with live web data. Export as PDF for board decks and strategy documents.

## SWOT Grid

Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats in a 2x2 visual.

Pre-research: Ultron searches competitor news, reviews, and pricing before populating. Only verified intel fills the cells.

**How to trigger:**
```text
Run a SWOT analysis on HubSpot.
```
```text
Create a SWOT grid for my pitch vs Salesforce.
```

---

## Positioning Map

Competitive positioning across two axes you define.

**Default axes:** Price (low to high) vs Feature depth (lightweight to enterprise). You can specify your own.

**What it shows:** Your product and competitors as positioned dots on the map. Visual gaps are visible immediately.

**How to trigger:**
```text
Create a positioning map: X-axis = price, Y-axis = AI-nativeness.
Products: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Ultron.
```

---

## Score Card

Weighted scoring across multiple criteria. You define the criteria and weights.

**When to use:** Vendor evaluation. Partner selection. Market entry decisions. Comparing options against your priorities.

**How to trigger:**
```text
Score HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive across:
Ease of use (30%), Integration depth (40%), Pricing (30%).
```

---

## Radar Chart

Multi-dimension capability comparison. 5–8 dimensions shown on a radar.

**What it shows:** Capability gaps and strengths at a glance. Each product is a polygon on the radar. Overlap and gaps are immediately visible.

**How to trigger:**
```text
Create a radar chart comparing our product vs HubSpot and Salesforce across:
Speed, AI capability, Integration depth, Price, Support quality, Ease of setup.
```

---

## Decision Tree

Branching logic framework for complex decisions.

**Inputs:** The decision question, branching criteria, outcomes at each branch

**When to use:** Lead qualification logic. Sales conversation routing. Internal process documentation. Pricing decision frameworks.

**How to trigger:**
```text
Create a decision tree for qualifying inbound leads
based on company size, industry, and stated budget.
```
