Five canvas templates for operational visibility and planning.

## Kanban Board

P0/P1/P2 task board with columns: inbox, in_progress, review, done, blocked.

**Shows:** Task cards with title, assignee, priority, and age. Current state snapshot rendered as a visual board.

**Data source:** Your current task queue in Ultron, or you can describe the tasks.

**How to trigger:**
```text
Show me the current task board.
```
```text
Create a project kanban for the Q2 launch.
```

---

## Timeline

Roadmap or milestone sequence with phases, dates, deliverables, and owners.

**When to use:** Project plans. Product roadmaps. Launch timelines. Onboarding sequences.

**How to trigger:**
```text
Create a launch timeline for our Q2 product release with milestones from now through June 30.
```

---

## Funnel Chart

Conversion funnel with stage values and drop-off rates.

**Stages:** Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Intent → Evaluation → Conversion (or your custom stages)

**Shows:** Count per stage, conversion rate from previous stage, deal value per stage

**How to trigger:**
```text
Show my sales pipeline as a funnel chart.
```
```text
Create a funnel chart: 200 leads → 80 contacted → 35 replied → 12 meetings → 4 closed.
```

---

## Metrics Dashboard

KPI cards with trend indicators — week-over-week or month-over-month.

**Cards:** Lead count, Emails sent, Reply rate, Meetings booked, Deals in pipeline, Revenue closed

**Data source:** Connected CRM, Stripe (if connected), or you describe the numbers.

**How to trigger:**
```text
Generate a metrics dashboard for this week.
```
```text
Show my sales KPIs: 24 leads, 18 emails, 11% reply rate, 3 meetings booked.
```

---

## Sequence Builder

Step-by-step workflow or email cadence visualized as a timeline.

**Shows:** Step number, action, timing, owner, status per step

**When to use:** Email sequence planning. Onboarding flow documentation. Sales process maps.

**How to trigger:**
```text
Build a sequence for my 5-email nurture campaign:
Day 0: welcome, Day 3: case study, Day 7: tactical value, Day 10: soft pitch, Day 14: follow-up.
```
