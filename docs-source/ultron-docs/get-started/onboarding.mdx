When you first chat with Ultron, it runs an onboarding sequence to learn about your business.

## What Ultron asks

### Business Profile
- What's your business name?
- What do you sell? (products, services, pricing)
- Who is your ideal customer? (ICP description)
- Who are your main competitors?
- What are common objections you hear?
- Do you have case studies or testimonials?

### Content Voice
- What platforms do you publish on? (LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, Substack, Blog, Email)
- What tone do you write in? (professional, casual, provocative, educational)
- Paste 2-3 examples of content you've written so Ultron can calibrate your voice

### Sales Context
- What's your average deal size?
- What's your sales cycle length?
- What verticals do you target?
- What job titles do you sell to?

## Where this data lives

All onboarding data is stored in your `profiles` table:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `business_name` | Company name |
| `business_description` | What you do |
| `icp_description` | Your ideal customer |
| `platforms` | Where you publish (array) |
| `voice_tone` | Your writing style |
| `voice_samples` | Example posts (array) |
| `business_profile` | Pricing, case studies, objections, competitors (JSON) |
| `onboarding_completed` | Whether onboarding is done |

## Re-running onboarding

Update your business profile anytime from **Dashboard → Settings → Business Profile**. Changes take effect immediately on your next conversation.
