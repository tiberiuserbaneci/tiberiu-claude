## ICP Definition

Your Ideal Customer Profile is the single most important input for Ultron Sales. Be specific.

<Warning>
**Bad:** "Business owners"

**Good:** "SaaS product managers at B2B companies with 50-500 employees in the US, Series A-B funded, using HubSpot or Salesforce"
</Warning>

Ultron uses your ICP to build Apollo.io search queries, score leads (0-100), write personalized outreach angles, and qualify prospects using BANT.

## Voice Calibration

Ultron Creates needs to sound like you. Provide:

1. **Tone descriptor:** "Direct, no-BS, action-oriented" or "Thoughtful, data-driven, contrarian"
2. **Voice samples:** Paste 2-3 of your best-performing posts. Ultron analyzes sentence length, vocabulary, hook patterns, and CTA style.
3. **Platform preferences:** Tell Ultron which platforms you prioritize.

## Business Intelligence

The more context Ultron has, the better Deal Closer performs:

- **Pricing:** What do you charge? Any tiers?
- **Case studies:** What results have clients achieved?
- **Objection map:** What pushback do you hear? What are the rebuttals?
- **Competitors:** Who do prospects compare you to?
- **Sales process:** How do deals flow from first contact to close?

## Updating Information

Say `"update my ICP"` or `"change my voice tone"` in any chat and Ultron will walk you through updating the relevant fields.
