## Research & Intelligence

_These go to Cortex. Expect real data from live sources — not summaries._

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Competitive audit">
    ```
    Audit [competitor URL] and find 5 weaknesses in their GTM strategy
    ```
    Cortex browses their site, reviews their messaging, cross-references reviews, and delivers specific vulnerabilities you can use in your own positioning.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Funding-based prospecting research">
    ```
    Find 10 companies in [industry] that raised Series A this month
    ```
    Cortex searches funding databases and startup news sources, then returns a list with company details, investor names, and founder contacts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pre-call prospect research">
    ```
    Research [prospect name] at [company] before my call tomorrow — company context, their role, recent posts, and likely pain points
    ```
    Cortex delivers a pre-call brief with everything you need to have a relevant conversation. Takes 3-4 minutes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Partnership discovery">
    ```
    Analyze ProductHunt launches this week and find 5 partnership opportunities for a [your business type]
    ```
    Cortex scrapes the week's launches, filters for products that serve your ICP without competing, and ranks them by partnership potential.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Competitor pricing comparison">
    ```
    Compare our pricing to [competitor 1], [competitor 2], [competitor 3] — pull their live pricing pages and build a comparison table
    ```
    Cortex visits each pricing page live and returns a clean comparison with your positioning relative to each.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Industry trend monitoring">
    ```
    What are the top 5 trends in [industry] right now based on recent content, funding activity, and social discussion?
    ```
    Cortex pulls from current sources — not training data — and returns an evidence-backed trend report with source links.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Thought leader discovery">
    ```
    Find [industry] thought leaders who are actively posting about [topic] and gaining traction
    ```
    Cortex identifies active voices in your space — useful for partnership targets, guest post opportunities, or competitor intel.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Lead Generation

_These go to Specter. Results land in your Leads table, scored and ready._

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="ICP-matched outreach">
    ```
    Find 5 [ICP description] companies and draft personalized outreach for each
    ```
    Replace [ICP description] with something specific: "B2B SaaS companies with 20-100 employees in the logistics space, Series A funded." The more specific, the better the leads.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Hiring signal targeting">
    ```
    Build a lead list of companies actively hiring [role] — they probably need [your solution]
    ```
    Example: "Build a lead list of companies hiring Sales Ops Managers — they probably need better pipeline visibility tools." Specter finds the signal and builds outreach around it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pain signal targeting">
    ```
    Find founders who posted about [pain point] on LinkedIn this week
    ```
    Example: "Find founders who posted about team collaboration problems on LinkedIn this week." These are warm targets — they're actively talking about the problem you solve.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Conference targeting">
    ```
    Find [industry] conferences happening next quarter and pull the speaker list — I want to reach out to speakers who match our ICP
    ```
    Specter finds events, pulls speaker info, filters for ICP fit, and drafts outreach that references their upcoming talk.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Competitor customer targeting">
    ```
    Find companies that are publicly using [competitor] — they're potential switchers
    ```
    Specter finds companies mentioning the competitor in job posts, case studies, or tech stacks, and flags them as targets for competitor displacement messaging.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Funding-based outreach">
    ```
    Find [job title] at companies that raised Series [A/B] in [industry] in the last 30 days and draft outreach
    ```
    Fresh money means active buying. Specter targets recently funded companies while the budget is being allocated.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Sales & Deals

_These go to Striker. Needs Gmail connected for inbox-related tasks._

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Inbox triage">
    ```
    Triage my inbox and flag any deal opportunities with suggested next steps
    ```
    Striker reads your Gmail, identifies warm leads and deal threads, and returns a prioritized action list with draft replies for each.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Context-aware follow-up">
    ```
    Draft a follow-up email to [name] referencing our last conversation about [topic]
    ```
    Striker searches your Gmail history with that person, reads the thread, and writes a follow-up that references the actual conversation — not a generic bump.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pre-demo research brief">
    ```
    Research [company] before my demo at [time] — find their tech stack, team size, recent funding, and top pain signals
    ```
    Striker pulls company intel from live sources and formats it as a 2-minute pre-call brief.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Competitive battle card">
    ```
    Create a battle card comparing us vs [competitor] for my sales call
    ```
    Striker + Cortex combine: Cortex audits the competitor live, Striker maps your strengths and counter-arguments, and formats it as a scannable battle card.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pipeline check">
    ```
    Which deals in my pipeline haven't had activity in more than 7 days?
    ```
    Striker reviews your pipeline and flags stale deals with suggested re-engagement messages.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Proposal generation">
    ```
    Write a proposal for [company name] — they need [use case], budget is around [range], decision maker is [title]
    ```
    Striker uses your business profile (pricing, case studies, differentiators) to write a tailored proposal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Content & Social

_These go to Pulse. Results land in Content Drops as drafts for review._

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Weekly LinkedIn posts">
    ```
    Write a week of LinkedIn posts about [topic] in my voice — mix of story, insight, and how-to formats
    ```
    Pulse generates 5 posts with varied angles, all calibrated to your voice samples and tone settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Customer win → content">
    ```
    Turn this customer win into a case study and LinkedIn post: [paste your customer story or result]
    ```
    Pulse structures it as Problem → Solution → Result, writes the long-form version, and extracts a punchy social post.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Twitter thread">
    ```
    Create a Twitter thread breaking down [industry trend or insight] — founder perspective, contrarian if the data supports it
    ```
    Pulse researches the topic live, then writes an 8-10 tweet thread with a scroll-stopping hook.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cold email templates">
    ```
    Write 3 cold email templates for [ICP] focusing on [pain point] — keep each under 100 words
    ```
    Pulse writes three angles: problem-aware, social-proof-led, and challenger. All short enough to actually get read.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Blog post">
    ```
    Draft a blog post about [topic] — 800 words, tactical, founder voice, include at least 3 specific examples
    ```
    Pulse researches the topic with Cortex, structures it with a clear narrative, and writes in your voice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Content repurposing">
    ```
    Repurpose this [LinkedIn post / blog post / newsletter] for Twitter and a cold email opener: [paste content]
    ```
    Pulse adapts format and length for each channel while keeping the core insight intact.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Newsletter section">
    ```
    Write a newsletter section about [topic] — 200 words, conversational, for a [audience type] audience
    ```
    Short enough to be a genuine newsletter section, not a blog post disguised as one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Operations & Monitoring

_These go to Sentinel. Runs health checks on live systems._

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Website health check">
    ```
    Run a full health check on [your domain] — speed, SSL, broken links, mobile
    ```
    Sentinel runs real HTTP checks and returns a severity-ranked report.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="API audit">
    ```
    Audit our API response times for these endpoints: [list endpoints] — flag anything over [X]ms
    ```
    Sentinel sends test requests and measures actual response times.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Competitor monitoring">
    ```
    Check if [competitor 1] and [competitor 2] changed their pricing pages this week
    ```
    Sentinel visits each page and reports any changes since last check.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SSL check">
    ```
    Check SSL expiry on [domain] and flag if it's expiring within 30 days
    ```
    A simple but important check — many teams miss SSL renewals until the site breaks.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Power Combos

_Multi-agent workflows. Send one prompt, multiple agents execute in sequence._

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Find → Research → Outreach pipeline">
    ```
    Find 5 [ICP description] leads → research each one → draft personalized outreach for each
    ```
    **How it works:**
    1. Specter finds and scores 5 matching companies
    2. Cortex researches each one for specific context
    3. Specter drafts outreach using Cortex's research to personalize each message

    **Output:** 5 enriched leads in your Leads table, each with a research-backed outreach draft.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Competitor audit → content → outreach">
    ```
    Audit [competitor URL] → write a comparison post highlighting where we win → create outreach for their customers
    ```
    **How it works:**
    1. Cortex audits the competitor and finds their weaknesses
    2. Pulse writes a comparison content piece that positions you against those weaknesses
    3. Specter finds companies using the competitor and drafts displacement outreach

    **Full competitive play in one prompt.**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Inbox triage → research → draft replies">
    ```
    Triage my inbox → identify the 3 warmest leads → research each company → draft personalized replies
    ```
    **How it works:**
    1. Striker reads your Gmail and flags warm opportunities
    2. Cortex pulls company context for each warm lead
    3. Striker drafts replies that reference both the email thread and the research

    **Never reply cold again.** Every follow-up is informed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Research → content campaign">
    ```
    Research the top 5 trends in [industry] this month → write a LinkedIn post about each one in my voice
    ```
    **How it works:**
    1. Cortex pulls current data on industry trends from live sources
    2. Pulse writes 5 posts, each tied to a real trend with real data

    **Content grounded in current facts — not generic takes.**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lead gen + content amplification">
    ```
    Find [ICP] founders who are active on LinkedIn → find what topics they post about → write 3 posts that speak directly to those themes
    ```
    **How it works:**
    1. Specter finds ICP-matched founders and identifies their content themes
    2. Pulse writes posts optimized to resonate with exactly that audience

    **Content that your target buyers are already interested in.**
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
