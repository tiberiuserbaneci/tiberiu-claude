Pulse plans, drafts, scores, and queues content across all your platforms. You review and approve. The calendar runs itself.

## The workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Profile drives everything">
    Before Pulse writes a word, it reads your profile: platforms (LinkedIn, Twitter, blog), voice tone, banned words, voice samples, and ICP.

    Pulse never writes content that violates your voice. The samples you provided are the baseline.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Trend research">
    ```text
    What are the biggest topics in B2B sales right now?
    What's contrarian? What's being overdone?
    ```

    Pulse runs web_search × 3 + Reddit research. Surfaces trending angles, contrarian takes, and what competitors are writing about (so you can zig when they zag).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Content calendar generated">
    ```text
    Create a 2-week content calendar for LinkedIn and Twitter.
    ```

    Content Calendar canvas generated: 14 rows, each with date, platform, format, topic, and hook draft.
  </Step>

  <Step title="You review the calendar">
    Approve topics, swap any you don't like, add your own. The calendar is editable inline.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pulse drafts each piece">
    For each approved topic, Pulse drafts the full post following platform rules. VIRALS scored. Revised if below 7. Returned when it passes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Content queued and published">
    Approved content saved to queue. Published on schedule via connected social integrations or downloaded for manual posting.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Performance tracked">
    Published pieces saved to memory. Pulse references top performers in future content cycles — format, angle, and hook type noted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 7-day rotation

| Day | Type | Why |
|-----|------|----||
| 1 | Controversial take | High engagement, generates comments |
| 2 | How-to / tactical | Saves, shares, follows |
| 3 | Story / narrative | Trust building, relatability |
| 4 | Listicle | Wide reach, low friction |
| 5 | Industry news angle | Timely, positions you as informed |
| 6 | Community engagement | Replies, visibility |
| 7 | Repeat best performer | Compound on what already works |
