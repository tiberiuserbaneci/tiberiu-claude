Ultron's subscription covers the platform. AI costs are separate.

## How it works

You bring your own Claude or OpenRouter API key. When Ultron makes an AI call, it uses YOUR key. The cost goes directly to your AI provider.

**We never mark up AI costs. We never charge you for AI usage.**
