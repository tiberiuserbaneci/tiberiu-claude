## Frequently asked pricing questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Free and Max in practice?">
    On Free, Ultron confirms what your agents would do but doesn't execute fully. On Max, agents execute completely — Specter actually saves leads to your table, Pulse actually drops content drafts, Striker actually reads your inbox. Free is for evaluating. Max is for working.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel anytime?">
    Yes. Cancel from Settings → Plan & Billing. Your workspace and data remain accessible. You drop back to Free limits (5 conversations/day, preview mode).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there an annual plan?">
    Annual pricing with a discount is available. Contact us for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my leads and content if I downgrade?">
    Your data stays in your workspace. You can still access and export everything. You just can't run new full-execution tasks on Free.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
