## Content Creation

| Command | Example |
|---|---|
| `"Write a [platform] post about [topic]"` | "Write a LinkedIn post about scaling without hiring" |
| `"Draft a [type] about [topic]"` | "Draft a Twitter thread about BYOK pricing" |
| `"Create a carousel about [topic]"` | "Create an Instagram carousel about founder burnout" |
| `"Write an article about [topic]"` | "Write a Substack article on AI agents" |
| `"Repurpose [content] for [platforms]"` | "Repurpose my LinkedIn post for Twitter and IG" |
| `"Write [number] posts about [topic]"` | "Write 5 posts about AI automation" |

## Research

| Command | Example |
|---|---|
| `"What's trending in [niche] on [platform]?"` | "What's trending in AI on LinkedIn?" |
| `"Scrape top posts about [topic]"` | "Scrape top 10 posts about founder-led growth" |
| `"Analyze this: [paste content]"` | Reverse-engineer any piece of content |
| `"Content calendar"` | "Generate a 30-day content calendar" |
| `"Content ideas for [topic]"` | "Give me 20 ideas about AI automation" |

## Management

| Command | Example |
|---|---|
| `"Show my drafts"` | List all draft content pieces |
| `"Approve [content]"` | Mark content as approved |
| `"Schedule [content] for [date]"` | "Schedule the post for tomorrow 9 AM" |
