## Voice Calibration

Ghost Writer uses your voice profile to match:
- Sentence length and rhythm
- Vocabulary and terminology
- Hook patterns (question, stat, story, contrarian)
- CTA style (soft ask, hard ask, no ask)
- Platform-specific formatting

## Platform Formatting

### LinkedIn
- Hook in first line (shown before "see more")
- Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences)
- Line breaks between paragraphs
- CTA at the end, 3-5 hashtags at bottom

### Twitter/X
- 280 character limit per tweet
- Threads: numbered (1/X format)
- Hook tweet is everything
- Last tweet = CTA

### Instagram
- Carousel: 8-10 slides, headline + supporting text per slide
- Caption: hook, body, CTA, hashtags
- Reel script: hook (first 3 seconds), body, CTA

### Substack / Blog
- Long-form (1,000-3,000 words)
- H2/H3 structure
- Internal CTAs

### Email Newsletter
- Subject line (4-7 words), preview text
- Personal opener, 1-3 key points, single CTA

## Example Prompts

```
"Write a LinkedIn post about scaling without hiring"
"Draft a Twitter thread on why AI agents replace SaaS"
"Create an Instagram carousel about founder burnout"
"Write a Substack article on BYOK pricing models"
"Repurpose my last LinkedIn post for Twitter and Instagram"
```
