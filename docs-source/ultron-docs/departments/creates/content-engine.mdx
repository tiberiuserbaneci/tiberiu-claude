## Pipeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trend Discovery">
    Searches for high-performing content matching your niche and platforms. If Apify is connected, deep-scrapes LinkedIn, Twitter, and Instagram for engagement data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pattern Analysis">
    For each piece, identifies: hook type, content structure, engagement triggers, CTA type, optimal length.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Template Extraction">
    Converts winning patterns into reusable templates with hook, structure, and CTA frameworks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Batch Generation">
    Using templates + your voice profile, generates original content in batches. One research session can produce 10-30 unique pieces.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 30-Day Content Calendar

Say **"Generate a 30-day content calendar"** and Ultron creates a full month:
- 5 posts/week minimum
- Mix of types (educational, storytelling, promotional, engagement)
- Platform-specific formatting
- Scheduled dates and times
- All stored in the Content Calendar
