## How it works

Ultron analyzes your voice samples to extract:

| Signal | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Sentence length | Average words per sentence, variation |
| Vocabulary | Technical vs casual, jargon usage |
| Hook patterns | How you open posts (question, stat, story, contrarian) |
| Structure | Paragraph length, use of lists, formatting habits |
| CTA style | Soft ask, hard ask, or no ask |
| Tone markers | Confident, humble, provocative, educational |

## Providing Voice Samples

Best results come from 2-3 of your **top-performing posts**. These should be:
- Posts you're proud of (they represent your best voice)
- Posts that got engagement (the audience responded to this voice)
- From the platform you use most

## Updating Your Voice

Say `"update my voice"` or go to **Settings → Business Profile → Voice** to:
- Change tone descriptor
- Add or replace voice samples
- Adjust platform priorities
