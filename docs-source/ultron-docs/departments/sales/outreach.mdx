## The 95/5 Rule

Ultron follows a strict ratio when writing outreach: 95% of the email is about the recipient, 5% is about you.

Most cold emails fail because they lead with the sender. They open with who they are, what they built, and why it is great. The prospect does not care. They care about their own problems.

Ultron researches each prospect before writing, finds the specific angle that will resonate, and builds an email that leads with their situation. Your offer appears at the end, as the natural solution.

## Writing Your First Outreach Email

The minimum context Ultron needs is: who you are emailing and what you offer.

```
Write a cold email to the leads in my dashboard.
I sell AI outreach automation that reduces SDR workload by 60%.
Target: B2B SaaS founders with a sales team.
```

Ultron will pull the leads from your dashboard, research each one individually, and write a personalized email for every contact. You will see each draft before anything is sent.

## Personalization Levels

You control how deep Ultron goes on personalization. The deeper the personalization, the longer each email takes to generate. Match the effort to the deal size.

**Level 1: ICP-level personalization**

Ultron tailors the email to the prospect's role, company size, and industry. Fast. Works for high-volume outreach.

```
Write 50 personalized cold emails for my lead list.
Keep it efficient. One angle per company type.
```

**Level 2: Company-level personalization**

Ultron researches each company specifically: recent funding, product changes, hiring signals, public content. Slower but higher response rate.

```
Write 10 personalized emails. Research each company before writing.
I want to reference something specific to their business in every email.
```

**Level 3: Contact-level personalization**

Ultron researches the individual: their LinkedIn activity, recent posts, interviews, public statements. Reserved for high-value, named accounts.

```
Write a cold email to [Name], CEO of [Company].
Research them specifically. I want to open with something they personally said or published recently.
This is a target account worth $50k ARR to me.
```

## Email Prompt Templates

### Standard cold outreach

```
Write a cold email to [Name] at [Company].
They are a [role] at a [size] [industry] company.
I offer [your offer in one sentence].
The main pain point I solve is [specific pain].
Keep it under 100 words. No fluff. End with one clear question.
```

### Trigger-based outreach (funding, hiring, news)

```
Write a cold email referencing that [Company] just raised $[X]M.
They will be scaling fast and have budget decisions to make.
I sell [offer]. Frame my outreach around helping them scale without chaos.
```

```
[Company] is currently hiring 3 SDRs. Write a cold email that acknowledges their growth
and positions my AI outreach tool as a force multiplier for the team they are building.
```

### After content or event interaction

```
[Name] just published a LinkedIn post about [topic].
Write an outreach email that references their specific point of view and connects it naturally
to the problem I solve. Do not be obvious about it.
```

### Re-engagement for cold leads

```
I emailed [Name] 30 days ago and got no reply.
Write a short re-engagement email that takes a completely different angle.
Do not reference the previous email. Fresh start.
```

## Follow-Up Sequences

Ultron can write full multi-step sequences, not just single emails. Specify how many touches and the spacing.

```
Write a 4-step email sequence for my SaaS founder leads.
Step 1: Cold intro, under 80 words, research-driven opening.
Step 2: Day 3 follow-up, different angle, add a data point or case study.
Step 3: Day 7 follow-up, very short, check-in tone.
Step 4: Day 14 breakup email. Make it clean and leave the door open.
```

## Approval Before Sending

Ultron will always show you the email drafts before sending anything. This is a hard rule built into the system.

After Ultron writes the emails, it will say:

```
Here are the 10 draft emails. Review them and confirm before I send.
Type "send all", "send [number]", or "edit [number]" to proceed.
```

You control what goes out. Nothing is sent without your confirmation.

```
Write 5 subject line variations for this email.
Test angles: curiosity, specificity, pain point, social proof, and directness.
```

## Tracking Replies

When a lead replies, Ultron logs the conversation in your dashboard under the lead's record. From there you can ask:

```
[Name] replied. Here is what they said: [paste reply].
Write a response that moves them toward a call.
```

```
Summarize all lead replies from this week and tell me which ones are sales-ready.
```
