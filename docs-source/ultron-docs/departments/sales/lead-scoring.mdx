## Score Range

Every lead gets a score from 0-100 based on three dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| **Firmographic Fit** | 40% | Company size, industry, location, funding stage match to ICP |
| **Behavioral Signals** | 30% | Recent LinkedIn activity, job changes, content engagement |
| **Timing Triggers** | 30% | Funding round, new hire, product launch, competitor switch |

## Score Tiers

| Score | Tier | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 80-100 | Hot | Immediate outreach, top priority |
| 60-79 | Warm | Personalized outreach within 48 hours |
| 40-59 | Cool | Add to nurture sequence |
| 0-39 | Cold | Monitor for timing triggers |

## BANT Qualification

For high-scoring leads, Ultron assesses:
- **Budget:** Can they afford your solution?
- **Authority:** Is this person a decision-maker?
- **Need:** Do they have a clear pain point you solve?
- **Timeline:** Are they ready to buy now or later?
