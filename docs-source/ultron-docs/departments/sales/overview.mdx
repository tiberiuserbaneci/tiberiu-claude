Ultron Sales combines two skills — **ProspectIQ Engine** and **Deal Closer** — to run your entire sales operation from one chat.

## What it does

- Find and qualify leads matching your ICP
- Enrich leads with multi-source research
- Score prospects 0-100 based on fit and timing
- Generate hyper-personalized cold outreach
- Track deals through a visual pipeline
- Generate proposals, handle objections, plan follow-ups
