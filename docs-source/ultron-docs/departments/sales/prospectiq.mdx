## What ProspectIQ Does

ProspectIQ is Ultron's lead generation engine. When you give it an Ideal Customer Profile, it searches the web in real time, finds matching companies and contacts, enriches each result with publicly available data, and delivers a scored, ranked list directly in your dashboard.

Ultron does not pull from a static database. Every search is live. This means results reflect what companies are actually doing right now, not six months ago.

---

## Your First Lead Search

The most important thing you can do is be specific. Ultron performs better the more context you give it. A vague prompt returns vague leads. A precise prompt returns leads you can actually use.

**Weak prompt:**
```
Find me some leads
```

**Strong prompt:**
```
Find 30 B2B SaaS founders in the US with 10 to 50 employees who are currently hiring for sales roles. 
They should be post-revenue and ideally Series A or bootstrapped. 
I sell AI outreach automation that reduces SDR workload by 60%.
```

Ultron will run multiple web searches, cross-reference results, and return a structured list with company name, founder name, estimated size, funding stage, and a relevance score for your offer.

## Lead Search Prompts by Use Case

### Finding founders by niche

```
Find 25 agency owners in the US running done-for-you social media management. 
They should have 2 to 10 employees and be actively posting on LinkedIn. 
I offer white-label AI content systems they can resell to their clients.
```

```
Find 20 e-commerce brand owners doing over $500k per year in revenue.
Focus on Shopify brands in the beauty or health space.
I sell AI-powered email marketing automation.
```

### Finding companies actively hiring

```
Find 15 B2B companies in the UK that are currently hiring SDRs or BDRs.
Company size should be 20 to 200 employees. SaaS or professional services only.
This signals they are investing in outbound sales, which is exactly who I target.
```

### Finding companies by tech stack or signal

```
Find 20 companies using HubSpot CRM with a sales team of 5 or more people.
They are likely paying for multiple sales tools and would consolidate to save cost.
I sell a unified AI sales assistant that replaces 3 tools they are already paying for.
```

### Finding leads by recent event

```
Find 15 companies that recently raised a Series A in the last 6 months in the HR tech or recruting space.
They will have budget and a mandate to scale fast. 
I offer AI hiring automation that cuts time-to-hire by 40%.
```

## Refining Your Search Mid-Session

You do not need to restart a search to refine it. Ultron holds context across your conversation. You can narrow, expand, or pivot from the same thread.

```
Remove anyone with fewer than 10 employees from that list.
```

```
Add a column showing whether each company has a blog or posts on LinkedIn.
I want to prioritize the ones who are already doing content marketing.
```

```
Filter to US and Canada only. Remove all European companies from this batch.
```

```
Score these leads against my ICP and sort by relevance score descending.
```

## Bulk Lead Generation

For larger batches, give Ultron the total volume and let it run in stages.

```
I need 100 leads this week. Break it into 4 batches of 25.
Start with batch 1: SaaS founders in the US, 10 to 50 employees, post-revenue.
I will confirm when I am ready for the next batch.
```

Ultron will complete each batch, log the results to your Leads dashboard, and wait for your confirmation before proceeding. This gives you a review point between batches.

## Research Mode: Deep Dive on a Single Company

Before a call or a high-value outreach, use Ultron to build a full intelligence brief on a specific company.

```
Research Acme Corp for me before my call tomorrow.
I need: what they do, who the key decision makers are, recent news or announcements,
any funding events in the last 12 months, and what their main pain points likely are
based on their current size and market position.
```

Ultron will search the web, compile everything into a structured brief, and surface the 2 to 3 most relevant angles for your opening conversation.

## What Gets Saved to Your Dashboard

Every lead Ultron finds is automatically saved to your Leads table with:

- Company name and website
- Contact name and role
- Enrichment data (size, funding, location, tech signals)
- Lead score (1 to 100) based on ICP match
- Source and date found
- Outreach status (not contacted, emailed, replied, in pipeline)

You can filter, sort, tag, and export from the dashboard at any time.
