## What it does

- Generate deal intelligence reports
- Map objections and prepare rebuttals
- Write proposals and SOWs
- Plan follow-up sequences
- Track deals through pipeline stages

## Objection Mapping

Tell Ultron about an objection and it generates:
- The real concern behind the objection
- 2-3 rebuttal angles with supporting evidence
- Suggested response email or talk track

## Proposal Generation

Say **"Generate a proposal for [company]"** and Ultron creates:
- Executive summary
- Problem statement (personalized from research)
- Proposed solution
- Pricing and timeline
- Case studies / social proof
- Next steps and CTA

## Follow-Up Sequences

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Initial outreach |
| Day 3 | Value-add follow-up (share relevant content) |
| Day 7 | Social proof follow-up (case study) |
| Day 14 | Break-up email (last chance) |

## Example Prompts

```
"What objections might Acme Corp have about our pricing?"
"Write a proposal for Acme Corp, they need an AI SDR"
"Plan a follow-up sequence for leads who didn't reply"
"Move the Acme deal to negotiation"
"What's my pipeline value this month?"
```
