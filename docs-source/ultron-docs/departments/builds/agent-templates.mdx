## AI SDR Agent
**Purpose:** Automated lead generation and outreach
**Tasks:** Search for leads matching ICP, enrich data, draft personalized emails, queue for approval
**Schedule:** Daily at 8 AM
**Integrations:** Apollo.io, Google Workspace, ZeroBounce

## AI Content Manager
**Purpose:** Daily content creation and publishing
**Tasks:** Research trending topics, write posts in your voice, schedule across platforms
**Schedule:** Daily at 9 AM
**Integrations:** Apify (optional)

## AI Account Manager
**Purpose:** Client relationship monitoring
**Tasks:** Review active client status, flag at-risk accounts, draft check-in emails
**Schedule:** Weekly, Monday morning
**Integrations:** Google Workspace, HubSpot

## AI Research Analyst
**Purpose:** Competitive intelligence and market monitoring
**Tasks:** Monitor competitor websites, track industry news, summarize key changes
**Schedule:** Daily at 7 AM
**Integrations:** Web search (built-in)

## AI Finance Agent
**Purpose:** Invoice and payment tracking
**Tasks:** Check outstanding invoices, send payment reminders, generate weekly reports
**Schedule:** Weekly, Friday
**Integrations:** Stripe, Google Workspace
